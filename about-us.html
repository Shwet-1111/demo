About Texas Tree Expert

Family-Owned Tree Experts Serving Houston Since 1990

For more than three decades, Texas Tree Expert has helped homeowners and businesses throughout Houston care for their trees and protect their properties.

Founded in 1990, we are a family-owned and operated tree company built on experience, dependable service, and a commitment to doing the job safely and correctly. From routine tree maintenance to complex removals and urgent tree concerns, our team brings practical knowledge and professional care to every property we serve.

ISA Certified Arborist Expert

With an ISA Certified Arborist on staff, Texas Tree Expert can provide more than tree work alone. We help customers understand the condition of their trees, identify potential concerns, and make informed decisions about trimming, removal, maintenance, and long-term tree care.

Comprehensive Tree Services

Our services include tree removal, tree trimming, stump grinding, land clearing, emergency tree service, and arborist evaluations for both residential and commercial properties throughout Houston and surrounding communities.

Our Commitment to You

As a licensed and insured company, we take the protection of your property seriously. Our goal is simple: provide knowledgeable recommendations, quality workmanship, and a straightforward experience from the initial estimate through project completion.
